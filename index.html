About The Museum

The North Carolina Museum of Natural Sciences is a museum in downtown Raleigh that explores the natural world through science, research, and hands-on experiences. Visitors can learn about North Carolina's wildlife, ecosystems, geology, and much more!

Whether you are interested in dinosaurs, animals, the ocean, or space, there is something to discover at the museum. Visitors can explore interactive exhibits, see live animals, learn about scientific research, and participate in educational programs.

Things To Do

There is plenty to see and do at the NC Museum of Natural Sciences. In the museum, you can:

  • Explore interactive exhibits
  • See live animals
  • Learn about scientific research
  • Participate in educational programs
  • Discover dinosaurs and fossils
  • Learn about the ocean and marine life

And more!

Planning Your Visit

The museum is open year-round, and admission is free. Check the museum's website for current hours, special exhibits, and events.
